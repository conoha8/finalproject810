About Overwatch

OVERWATCH 2

Team-based multiplayer action — Blizzard Entertainment, 2022

Overwatch is a team-based multiplayer first-person shooter developed and published by Blizzard Entertainment. First released in May 2016, the game pits two teams of six players against each other across a variety of maps. Players choose from over 30 heroes, each with unique abilities, divided into three roles: Tank, Damage, and Support.


The story is set roughly 60 years in the future. Following a global crisis known as the Omnic Crisis, a UN strike force called Overwatch was formed. Though eventually disbanded, its heroes continue to fight for a better world.
