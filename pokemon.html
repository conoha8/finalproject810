About Pokémon Violet

POKÉMON VIOLET

Generation IX open-world RPG — Game Freak / Nintendo, 2022

Pokémon Violet is a 2022 role-playing game developed by Game Freak for the Nintendo Switch. It is part of Generation IX and introduces an open-world structure set in the Spain-inspired Paldea region.


Players ride the legendary Pokémon Miraidon across three story paths: Victory Road (Gym Leaders), Starfall Street (Team Star), and Path of Legends (Titan Pokémon). Over 100 new Pokémon were introduced, along with the Terastal phenomenon which allows type changes.
